The problem statement for the rest of the video: **sharing without coordination**.

person a tiny "?" bubble. The cell tower on the roof has no "conductor" or traffic-cop figure, just a mast.

curved into a circle or kept as a strip. There's no central controller: a dashed empty circle in the middle, labelled
"no one in charge", fades out to nothing.

## Bridge: real life → theory
